Verona grew fast, and the timing shows in its kitchens. A large share of the homes we work in - Cathedral Point, Sugar Creek, Scenic Ridge, Kettle Creek - were built from the early 2000s onward. That means cabinet boxes with real service life ahead of them, decent drawer hardware in the later builds, and layouts designed for open-plan living. Structurally, there is rarely anything wrong. What brings people to us is that a light maple or medium-stain cabinet package chosen by a builder fifteen or twenty years ago now reads as unmistakably of its era.

That makes Verona projects overwhelmingly style-driven, and refacing is well suited to it. New doors and drawer fronts in a painted shaker, with face frames and exposed end panels veneered to match, gives you the current look completely - and because we are not repairing damage or correcting a bad layout, these projects tend to run smoothly and finish on the early end of our three-to-five-day range.

Open-plan kitchens raise the stakes on finish choice

Verona floor plans usually put the kitchen in direct sight of the great room, which means your cabinet color is effectively the main color of the first floor. We take that seriously at the sample stage. We bring physical door samples and look at them against your flooring, your countertops, and the wall color in the adjoining living space, at the time of day you actually use the room. Two-tone treatments work particularly well in these layouts: warm white perimeter cabinetry with a deeper island in navy, forest green, or a natural wood tone reads intentional and keeps a large open space from feeling flat.

Storage in newer homes

Newer does not mean well-organized. Builder cabinet packages in these homes are usually generous in linear footage and thin on interior function - big shelved base cabinets, an underbuilt island interior, and a blind corner that swallows appliances. Converting shelves to full-extension pull-outs, adding a drawer bank, fitting a proper trash pull-out into the island, and extending cabinetry into the dead space above the uppers are the modifications we bundle into Verona refacing projects most often.

Working around Verona schedules

A lot of our Verona customers work on tight professional schedules, so we do early-morning and early-evening measures, and we are explicit up front about what days the crew will be in the house. Because refacing leaves your plumbing and appliances connected, you can host, cook, and work from home through the project. You will have a written quote in hand after the measure, with door style, finish, and hardware specified.
