McFarland is a small village with a specific wrinkle: a good share of the homes we work in are on or near Lake Waubesa. That matters for cabinetry more than people expect. Lake-adjacent kitchens run at higher ambient humidity through the warm months, then swing to very dry indoor air once the furnace runs all winter. Wood moves with that cycle. Door constructions and finishes that shrug it off are not the same ones we would spec for an inland subdivision, and getting this wrong is how you end up with hairline cracks opening at the joints of a painted door after one Wisconsin heating season.

For those homes we lean toward door constructions and finishes engineered to tolerate movement, and we are frank about the tradeoffs between a painted five-piece door, a wood-grain laminate surface, and a real-wood veneer in a high-humidity setting. If a house is a seasonal or part-time residence and goes unconditioned for stretches, that changes the recommendation again, and it is worth telling us at the measure.

The housing mix

Away from the shoreline, McFarland is largely 1960s through 1990s ranches, split-levels, and raised ranches, plus newer construction toward the Yahara Hills side. The older ranches typically still have birch or oak cabinetry on plywood boxes - excellent refacing candidates with layouts that generally function fine for their size. Lake cottages and homes that have been expanded over the decades are the more interesting cases: cabinetry added in phases across different eras, so the kitchen is a patchwork of finishes and door styles that do not match. Refacing solves that specific problem better than anything else, because every visible surface ends up in one consistent finish regardless of when each cabinet was installed.

Hard water and daily wear

Dane County water is hard, and in kitchens it shows up as mineral spotting and film on cabinet surfaces near the sink and dishwasher. It is worth knowing before you pick a finish: high-gloss surfaces show water spotting and fingerprints far more than a satin or matte finish does. When we bring samples out, we point out which options will look better with less maintenance in the sink run specifically.

Working in McFarland

McFarland is a short drive from Madison and inside our standard service area with no travel surcharge. Village kitchens tend to be modest in size, so many McFarland projects install in two to four days rather than a full week. As everywhere, we measure every opening individually - important in homes that have been added onto - and hand you a written quote specifying door style, finish, and hardware before any work is scheduled.
